FAQs

Your questions,
answered.

A little clarity before you choose the right module for your team.

Can I buy this module on its own?

Yes. This module can be purchased separately, so you can start with the feature your business needs without purchasing a complete HRMS plan.

How is the module price calculated?

Your monthly total equals the base price per employee multiplied by your selected employee count. For example, an illustrative PKR 50 rate for 50 employees gives a monthly module total of PKR 2,500.

Can I change the employee count?

Use the slider or enter your headcount directly to see a new estimate. For employee-count changes on an active subscription, contact Onyx to confirm the applicable billing process.

Can I try the module before buying?

Yes. Try Live opens the original Onyx guest workspace, where you can explore the system using fictional demonstration records.

Can I add other modules later?

You can explore and purchase additional Onyx modules separately. Contact Onyx to confirm the right combination and setup for your business.

What happens when I click Buy Now?

You’ll continue to the Onyx get-started page with this module and your selected employee count included in the link. This template does not process payments, create a subscription or confirm a purchase.
