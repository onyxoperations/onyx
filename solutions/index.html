01 / Structured organizations

Corporates & Enterprises

Departments, reporting lines and sensitive people data need structure—not more disconnected spreadsheets. For corporate HR teams, Onyx brings employee administration, attendance, leave, payroll and recruitment into one workspace. HR can maintain consistent records while department managers and employees work within the access assigned to them.

A clearer organizational picture

Maintain employee profiles, departments and reporting relationships together, so HR has a dependable reference for who works where and who manages whom.

Controlled access to people data

Use role-based access to separate HR administration from employee self-service. Assign permissions intentionally rather than sharing unrestricted personnel files.

Connected monthly administration

Review attendance and leave alongside payroll inputs, adjustments and salary slips. Keep the underlying employee records consistent across the monthly HR cycle.

Consistent hiring and documentation

Manage recruitment records and use document templates for employment letters. Store personnel files centrally instead of recreating the same paperwork in each department.

A practical Onyx workflow

A corporate HR team reviews attendance, confirms approved leave and payroll adjustments, then generates salary slips while employees access their own information through the portal.
